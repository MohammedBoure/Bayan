# Curriculum Packs Directory (`lib/data/packs`)

This directory houses modular, extensible curriculum content packs organized into educational units, lessons, and interactive activities.

## Files:
- [grade3_curriculum_pack.dart](file:///C:/Users/moham/Desktop/Bayan/lib/data/packs/grade3_curriculum_pack.dart): Authentic 3rd Grade primary curriculum pack ingested directly from the official syllabus (`دروس السنة الثالثة ابتدائي.docx`). Implements the complete 5-stage pedagogical architecture: (1) Reading passages ("خدمة الأرض", "عمر ياسف", "من أجلك يا جزائر"), (2) Vocabulary glossaries & antonyms with textbook synonym substitution, (3) Comprehension discussion questions with model answers, (4) Inductive grammar observation ("ألاحظ وأميز") with explicit target words highlighted in red across all lessons, and (5) Rules with interactive whiteboard sentence parsing (including all 5 authentic present verb examples: سأرتدي البدلة، سأحمل رايتي، أطوف بها، أردد أناشيد الحرية، الحرية تستحق أن نحتفل بها) and 16 applied interactive activities.
- [grade4_curriculum_pack.dart](file:///C:/Users/moham/Desktop/Bayan/lib/data/packs/grade4_curriculum_pack.dart): Grade 4 syllabus covering subject and object inflection cases across singular, dual, and plural forms.
- [grade5_curriculum_pack.dart](file:///C:/Users/moham/Desktop/Bayan/lib/data/packs/grade5_curriculum_pack.dart): Grade 5 syllabus covering subjunctive and jussive particles for present verbs and advanced syntax.
