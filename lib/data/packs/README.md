# Curriculum Packs Directory (`lib/data/packs`)

This directory houses modular, extensible curriculum content packs organized into educational units, lessons, and interactive activities.

## Files:
- [grade3_curriculum_pack.dart](file:///C:/Users/moham/Desktop/Bayan/lib/data/packs/grade3_curriculum_pack.dart): Authentic 3rd Grade primary curriculum pack ingested directly from the official syllabus (`دروس السنة الثالثة ابتدائي.docx`). Implements the complete 5-stage pedagogical architecture: (1) Reading passages ("خدمة الأرض", "عمر ياسف", "من أجلك يا جزائر"), (2) Vocabulary glossaries & antonyms with textbook synonym substitution, (3) Comprehension discussion questions with model answers, (4) Inductive grammar observation ("ألاحظ وأميز") with explicit target words highlighted in red across all lessons, and (5) Rules with interactive whiteboard sentence parsing (including all 5 authentic present verb examples: سأرتدي البدلة، سأحمل رايتي، أطوف بها، أردد أناشيد الحرية، الحرية تستحق أن نحتفل بها) and 16 applied interactive activities.
- [grade4_curriculum_pack.dart](file:///C:/Users/moham/Desktop/Bayan/lib/data/packs/grade4_curriculum_pack.dart): Authentic 4th Grade primary curriculum pack ingested directly from the official syllabus (`دروس السنة الرابعة ابتدائي.docx`). Implements the complete 5-stage pedagogical architecture: (1) Reading passages ("التاجماعت", "المعلم الجديد", "بين جارين") with multi-track teacher audio narration and synchronized paragraph spotlighting (`assets/sounds/4_1/`, `4_2/`, `4_3/`), (2) Vocabulary glossaries (authentic «رصيدي الجديد» for all lessons, e.g., Lesson 2: حفاوة، الهندام، الوقار; Lesson 3: الشقة، المبيض), expressions, antonyms, textbook synonym substitutions, interactive word-meaning matching exercises («اختر لكل كلمة معناها» with word bank and tap-to-reveal matches), and the «أثري لغتي» linguistic enrichment sections (Lesson 1: complementary word pairs and morphological derivation tables; Lesson 2: odd-one-out exercises «عين العنصر الدخيل في كل سطر»; Lesson 3: complementary antonym pairs and morphological derivations for اسم الفاعل / المبالغة / المفعول), (3) Comprehension discussion questions with model answers, (4) Inductive grammar observation ("ألاحظ وأكتشف" with authentic trigger sentences, crimson target word highlights, and dedicated observation questions), and (5) Rules with interactive whiteboard sentence parsing and 21 applied interactive activities across Lessons 1, 2, and 3 (featuring 5 authentic classroom activities for Lesson 3: sentence target tap for direct objects, open sentence fill without suggestions, sentence syntax 3-part decomposition, written parsing with accusative helper chips, and reading story in-text direct object extraction).
- [grade5_curriculum_pack.dart](file:///C:/Users/moham/Desktop/Bayan/lib/data/packs/grade5_curriculum_pack.dart): Grade 5 syllabus covering subjunctive and jussive particles for present verbs and advanced syntax.
